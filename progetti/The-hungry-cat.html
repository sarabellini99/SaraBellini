THE HUNGRY CAT

The video playfully tells the story of the "buttered cat paradox": what happens if you put a slice of buttered toast on the back
of a cat? If toast always lands butter side down and a cat always lands on its feet, then neither
of them will ever touch the ground first, and you get perpetual motion.

The project was made entirely in 3D
using Blender.
