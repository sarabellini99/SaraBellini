PLASTIZ

Plastiz is a social impact startup founded in Turin in 2021, producing recycled plastic panels
for indoor and outdoor furniture. For two years I worked with the company, managing its communication.

On the digital side, I looked after the brand's presence on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and Pinterest, writing the editorial plan and creating the content: furniture photo shoots, videos
for social media and corporate videos. I also took care of updating the website and writing the monthly
newsletter. Offline, I designed
communication materials such as catalogs, business cards, brochures and event flyers.
