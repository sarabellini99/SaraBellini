REALISTA

Housing is not yet a right recognized by the Italian Constitution.
Meanwhile, rents in Italy's main cities keep rising. Realista is a social communication campaign
promoting the petition “Ma quale casa?” (“What house?”),
to make housing a constitutional right. The ironic
and provocative concept aims for virality to spread the message
and actively involve people.
