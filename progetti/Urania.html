URANIA

This editorial project is an experiential story about
movable type printing. By discovering the Antica Tipografia Portoghese, founded in 1891, the reader connects
with a tradition that lives on in the oldest letterpress workshop in Puglia. The book, divided into three chapters, guides the user through an interactive journey to
try movable type printing firsthand, exploring its processes,
gestures and the values that hold its essence.

PUBLISHING

2025

Let me introduce myself: I'm Urania, an old cylinder press from the Antica Tipografia Portoghese.

THE WEIGHT OF WORDS

The first chapter reflects on the relationship between signified
and signifier: eight words describe different sound intensities, from the extreme of silence to the opposite extreme
of noise. In the interactive part, the user learns to recognize
the visual weight of letters by matching
words to typeface weights.

THE CHARACTER OF TIME

The user faces the challenge of choosing what to print: the letters, taken out of the drawer, find their place
and, together, their meaning. In the interactive section, the whole printing process comes to life, through the slowness that
composing and locking up the forme demand.

THE PRESENCE OF IMPERFECTION

The user explores the marks that time has left on the wooden
type, rediscovering the uniqueness of imperfection. In the second part, starting from an imperfect, “lacking” letter, they are invited to let their imagination run free and create a new story from that unusual piece.

ANTICA TIPOGRAFIA PORTOGHESE

The graphics in the book take shape
from compositions and print runs made especially
for the project by the Antica Tipografia Portoghese. Special thanks to Gennaro, a printer since 1961, for his invaluable contribution.

Group project with Ilaria Iside Sabbà and Agnese Prieto
