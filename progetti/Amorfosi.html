AMORFOSI

A-morfosi is a sensory journey through the evolution of the shape of the letter A across history. The video alternates typefaces that, over time, have given it different interpretations: each one, iconic of its own time, comes to represent the era in which it was born.

The power of typography lies in its ability to shape identity
and convey feelings to the unconscious. The letter A turns
into a living sign: it takes on a personality and becomes the star of different stories, while remaining, deep down, always the same.
